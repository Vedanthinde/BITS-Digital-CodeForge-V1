## Short Project Summary

BITS Digital CodeForge V1.0 transforms the supplied grading-console
prototype into a cleaner and more reliable instructor workflow.

The application supports Excel marks upload, validation, course
selection, course analytics, configurable grade ranges, student-level
grade verification, finalization confirmation and CSV export.

## Three Meaningful Enhancements

1.  **Professional UI/UX** --- redesigned the interface into a clear,
    responsive workflow that makes grading steps easier to understand
    and use.
2.  **Stronger validation and error handling** --- improved Excel
    validation, marks validation, duplicate handling and grade-range
    checks.
3.  **Improved export and auditability** --- added grading-session
    context such as the generated timestamp and grading scale to the
    final export.

## Product Workflow

**Upload → Validate → Review Analytics → Configure Grades → Verify
Students → Finalize → Download CSV**

## Key Design Principle

The product was intentionally kept simple. The focus was on making the
existing grading workflow faster, clearer, safer and easier to verify
rather than adding unnecessary features.

## Disclaimer

This is a prototype created for the CodeForge challenge. It is not an
official BITS Pilani Digital grading system and is not intended for
actual academic grading.
